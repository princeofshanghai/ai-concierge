# Premium Plan Copy Reference

Concise reference for the 5 LinkedIn Premium products based on the screenshots provided.

Use this as the working source for recommendation-card copy and future side-panel content.

## Career

- Positioning: `Get hired and get ahead`
- Core idea: best for job seekers who want stronger visibility, better job targeting, and direct outreach tools
- Key benefits:
  - See jobs where you are likely to be a top applicant based on your skills
  - Improve your chances of hearing back by marking top-choice jobs
  - Message hiring managers directly with `5 InMail credits per month`

## Business

- Positioning: `Grow your network and find the right people`
- Core idea: best for professionals who want to build relationships, expand visibility, and access business insights
- Key benefits:
  - Find industry contacts and decision-makers with `unlimited people browsing`
  - Stand out with `exclusive profile customizations` and a `custom call to action`
  - Access growth and hiring trends with `company insights`

## All-in-One

- Positioning: `Sell, market, and hire in one tool`
- Core idea: broadest option for someone who wants one plan that supports client growth, profile reach, and hiring
- Key benefits:
  - Find new clients with `daily prospect suggestions` and `exclusive client insights`
  - Expand profile reach with `monthly post boosts` and `30 InMail credits per month`
  - Get qualified applicants with `monthly job promotions`

## Sales Navigator Core

- Positioning: `Drive more leads and sales opportunities`
- Core idea: dedicated selling tool for lead generation, outreach, and lead discovery
- Key benefits:
  - Find new leads with `50+ search filters`
  - Start more conversations with `50 InMail credits per month`
  - Save time with `smart lead recommendations` and `saved searches`

## Recruiter Lite

- Positioning: `Find and hire talent`
- Core idea: dedicated hiring tool for sourcing, outreach, and candidate discovery
- Key benefits:
  - Find qualified candidates faster with `20+ advanced search filters`
  - Get more responses with `30 InMail credits per month`
  - Save time with `daily candidate recommendations` and `search alerts`

## Quick Mapping

- `Career`: job search and applicant advantage
- `Business`: networking, visibility, and business discovery
- `All-in-One`: broad coverage across selling, marketing, and hiring
- `Sales Navigator Core`: dedicated selling workflow
- `Recruiter Lite`: dedicated hiring workflow

## Notes For Prototype Copy

- `All-in-One` should read as the broadest and most flexible option
- `Business` should read as the narrower networking and relationship-building option
- `Recruiter Lite` should read as the narrower hiring-focused option
- `Sales Navigator Core` and `Recruiter Lite` are better described as dedicated tools
